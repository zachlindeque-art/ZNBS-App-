Find a blood centre

ZNBTS has a blood centre in every province. In Lusaka it's at UTH on Nationalist Road. Pick your town, or use your location, to see the closest one first.

Mobile blood drives

A study at the Lusaka centre found about 90% of its blood came from mobile drives at schools, colleges and universities. If your school, church or workplace wants a drive, ask your nearest centre.
