# Zambia Blood Donor (proof of concept)

An independent prototype of a blood donor app for Zambia. It is not made by, endorsed by or connected to the Zambia National Blood Transfusion Service (ZNBTS).

- Map of the towns where ZNBTS runs provincial blood centres
- Log donations and get reminded every 6 months (calendar file, Google Calendar, browser notifications)
- English, Icibemba and Chinyanja

The whole app is one file, `index.html`. Donation history is stored only in the visitor's browser.

Live site: https://zachlindeque-art.github.io/ZNBS-App-/
