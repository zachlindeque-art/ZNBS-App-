      Before you donate

      Giving blood takes about an hour, and the needle part only around ten minutes. Here is what to expect. The team at the centre checks every donor and makes the final decision.

      Who can usually give

      • You are aged 16 to 65
      • You weigh at least 50 kg
      • You feel well on the day
      • You consider your blood safe to give to a patient

      On arrival the team checks your weight, blood pressure and haemoglobin (iron level) with a quick finger prick.

      Before you go

      • Eat a proper meal in the 3 to 4 hours before
      • Drink plenty of water
      • Avoid alcohol the day before
      • Carry your NRC or another photo ID
      • Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily

      After you donate

      • Rest for 10 to 15 minutes and take the drink and snack offered
      • Drink extra fluids for the next 24 hours
      • Skip heavy lifting and hard exercise for the rest of the day
      • If you feel faint, sit or lie down with your feet raised
      • Log the donation here so your reminder stays accurate

      Check with the team first if you

      • Have a fever, cough or feel unwell
      • Have had malaria recently
      • Are pregnant or have recently given birth
      • Had a tattoo, piercing, surgery or a transfusion recently
      • Take regular medication

      About this app

      This is an independent prototype of a blood donor app. It is not made by, endorsed by or connected to the Zambia National Blood Transfusion Service (ZNBTS). The map shows the towns where ZNBTS runs provincial blood centres. Confirm exact addresses, phone numbers and opening hours with ZNBTS before you travel. Your donation history and reminder settings are stored only in this browser and are never sent anywhere.
