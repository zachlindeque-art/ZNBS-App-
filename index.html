Your visit, about an hour

  1. 1

    Sign in5–10 min

    Show your NRC and fill in a short form.

  2. 2

    Quick check10–15 min

    Weight, blood pressure and a finger prick for iron.

  3. 3

    Giveabout 10 min

    About 450 ml. You'll feel a quick pinch at the start.

  4. 4

    Rest and snack10–15 min

    Sit for a while and have the drink and snack they give you.
